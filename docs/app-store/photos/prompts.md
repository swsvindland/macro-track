Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: fictional demo progress photo inside the VECTOR BODY App Store screenshot
Primary request: Create a natural phone-camera progress photograph of one fictional adult man, about 30 years old, standing relaxed facing the camera. An ordinary moderately muscular build with some softness around the waist; realistic, non-extreme starting physique. Plain charcoal athletic shorts, bare upper torso, no jewelry, no logos. Neutral light-gray wall, soft even daylight, eye-level tripod, arms slightly apart, neutral expression. Frame the full head through mid-shin, straight-on, portrait 2:3. Natural skin texture and believable anatomy. No text, no collage, no watermark, no dramatic lighting or flexing. This depicts a fictional model, not a real testimonial.

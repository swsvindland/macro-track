## Reply after the updated build is uploaded and verified

Thank you for the feedback. We added a Sources & methods screen accessible directly from the top of Overview and from every Overview calculation’s help popover, including when no measurements have been entered.

The screen provides clickable citations to CDC guidance for BMI, the published circumference-based body-fat equations in AR 600–9 (2019), ACE body-fat categories, and Schutz et al. (2002) for FFMI. It explains how the app uses trend weight and recorded or estimated body fat, and distinguishes the app’s simplified FFMI comparison bands and physique goal from clinical thresholds or medical recommendations.

It also states that the information is for general tracking, not diagnosis or treatment, and advises consulting a healthcare professional before making medical decisions.

To verify: open Overview → Sources & methods, then tap any publication title to open its source.

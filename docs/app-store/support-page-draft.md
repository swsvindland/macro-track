# VECTOR BODY support

Track weight, body measurements and progress photos on your iPhone.

For help, contact **[support email]**. Include your app version, iOS version and a short description of the issue. Please avoid sending private progress photos or Health records unless they are essential to your request.

## Where is my data stored?

Entries and photos are stored locally in the app. VECTOR BODY does not provide its own cloud backup. Device backups depend on your operating-system settings. Deleting the app may remove local records and photos.

## How do I change units or appearance?

Open Settings. Choose metric, imperial or decimal stone; choose light, dark or system appearance. Language is separate from units.

## How do I correct an entry?

Find the entry in its History list and tap its edit icon. For photos, tap the image, then Edit. You can change a date or delete a saved entry. Imported Health entries are managed by their original source.

## How does Apple Health sync work?

Enable Sync health data in Settings and grant the permissions you want to allow. Weight and height sync in both directions. Recorded body-fat percentages and waist circumference are exported. Progress photos, other tape measurements and calculated estimates stay in the app. iOS controls background timing, and read permissions can limit the records available to import.

## Why is trend weight different from my latest weight?

Trend weight smooths daily averages with a seven-day half-life. It helps show direction across everyday fluctuations; it is not your latest individual reading.

## Are body-fat and BMI values medical advice?

No. These are general adult reference tools and estimates. Circumference estimates depend on your measurements and the selected equation. A recorded body-fat percentage takes precedence over an estimate. Consult a qualified professional for medical guidance.

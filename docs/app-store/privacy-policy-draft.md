VECTOR BODY stores the weight entries, height entries, body measurements, preferences and progress photos you add in the app's local storage on your device. You do not need an account. The current app does not operate a developer-hosted account or cloud-storage service for these records.

## Photos

If you choose to take a progress photo, the app requests camera permission. You may also choose images from your photo library. Selected images are copied to the app's private document storage. You can delete saved photos in the app. Progress photos are not sent to Apple Health.

## Apple Health

Health integration is optional. With your authorization, the app reads weight and height from Apple Health and writes weight, height, recorded body-fat percentages and waist circumference to Apple Health. Calculated estimates and other circumference measurements are not exported. You control Health permissions in iOS. Apple's services and any other apps you authorize are subject to their respective privacy practices.

Disabling sync stops scheduled integration; it does not erase previously saved Health records. Imported records remain managed by their original source. Deleting an imported record in VECTOR BODY hides it locally without deleting the source record. Deleting an app-origin measurement queues deletion of its linked Health record for the next successful sync.

## Retention and device backups

Records remain in local app storage until deleted. The app does not provide its own cloud backup. Operating-system backups may include app data depending on your device settings. Removing the app may remove its local records and photos. Backup retention is controlled by the operating system and your backup provider.

## Contact and changes

For questions about privacy, contact [contact email]. We will update this policy when the app's data practices change.

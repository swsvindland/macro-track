# App Review notes

VECTOR BODY is a local-first app for recording weight, height, body circumferences and progress photos. No account or sign-in is required. There are no in-app purchases in the current implementation.

To review:

1. Open Overview and scroll to Add · Weight. Save an entry with today's date.
2. Open Height and add a height. Return to Overview to see BMI.
3. Open Body and add measurements. Neck and abdomen plus height support the male circumference equation; neck, waist and hips plus height support the female equation selected in Settings. A recorded body-fat percentage takes precedence over an estimate.
4. Open Photos, tap Add · Photos, choose a pose and date, then take or select a photo. Tap a saved image to preview or edit it.
5. In Settings, try units, language and appearance. Apple Health sync is optional and requests authorization when enabled. Weight and height sync in both directions; recorded body fat and waist circumference are exported. Photos and calculated estimates are not exported.

Health values are adult references and estimates, not a diagnosis. Background sync timing is controlled by iOS. Read access may be denied without HealthKit revealing the denial to the app.

Store screenshots use fictional sample records. Any synthetic progress images are illustrative and do not depict a real customer's results or promise a 90-day outcome.

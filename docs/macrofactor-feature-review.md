# MacroFactor feature review and Macro Track corrections

Reviewed official MacroFactor Nutrition documentation on September 25, 2026. This maps documented behavior, not their proprietary implementation. Their separate Workouts app is outside this comparison.

| Area             | MacroFactor's documented experience                                                                                                        | Macro Track direction                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Program creation | Guided coached, collaborative or manual programs; automatic initial energy/macros; diet/protein preferences; weekly calorie distribution   | Coached creation must be the primary path. Manual remains optional. Even daily budgets, or optional calorie shifting; collaborative budgets remain for later. |
| Goals            | Desired weight, gain/loss pace, maintenance weight; changes retain history and expenditure learning                                        | Cut/Bulk/Maintain, target weight and pace; preserve learned estimates across revisions.                                                                       |
| Weight           | Raw scale data plus a smoothed trend; recent information is weighted more strongly                                                         | Use the same normalized weight calculation in Progress and coaching. Individual scale spikes should not dictate targets.                                      |
| Adaptation       | Expenditure inferred from actual food intake and weight trend; adjustments do not require perfect adherence; smoothing limits overreaction | Calculate targets from the goal and expenditure, rather than penalizing missed calorie targets. Recalculate protein separately from carbs/fat.                |
| Maintenance      | Compare trend with selected maintenance weight and gently correct drift                                                                    | Use a target band and small corrections; reaching a cut/bulk goal stops further movement in that direction.                                                   |
| Check-ins        | Weekly configurable day; accept/decline; modules for partial logs, fasting, weigh-ins and breaks                                           | Keep explicit review, explain evidence/holds, recognize fasting, preserve learning during gaps.                                                               |
| Diary            | Hourly timeline, editable time/date, hide empty hours, configurable visible range/timestamps; copy/paste/move                              | Replace the four fixed meal buckets with timed entries and hourly groups. Preserve legacy untimed entries explicitly.                                         |
| Fast logging     | Barcode, search, hourly go-tos, multi-add plate, recent/favorite foods, quick add, quantity units, log by calories/macros                  | Existing offline search/scanning/quick-add/reuse remain; multi-add and hourly ranking are gaps.                                                               |
| Foods/recipes    | Personal foods, cooked yield, prepare/explode/duplicate recipes, shared recipes, URL/AI import                                             | Existing snapshot recipes and batch mass cover the basics; explode/import/sharing remain gaps.                                                                |
| Assisted logging | Describe/AI food logging, label scanner                                                                                                    | Local-only prototypes needed; no cloud fallback.                                                                                                              |
| Analytics        | Expenditure and weight-trend history, goal progress, weekly intake, energy balance, nutrients/timing/completeness                          | Expose plan, normalized weight and estimate first; deeper expenditure/nutrient analytics remain work.                                                         |
| Other            | Notes, body metrics/photos, period tracking, steps, health integrations, export, widgets/watch/shortcuts                                   | Keep nutrition focus. Existing weight/health/export foundations remain; most accessory features are later work.                                               |

The previous implementation had a material product gap: it asked for manual targets before coaching, required 21 perfect days, reset calibration on goal changes, and scaled all macros together. Those were prototype choices, not faithful representations of MacroFactor.

## Key findings and sources

- [Program styles](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/91-program-styles): coached generates and adjusts the program; collaborative retains user control over daily distribution; manual leaves targets to the user.
- [Program preferences](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/34-what-are-the-different-program-options-in-coached-mode): diet style, protein and calorie shifting are distinct choices.
- [Expenditure](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/20-expenditure): learning uses actual intake and changes in stored energy inferred from weight, independently of adherence to recommendations.
- [Weight trend](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/dashboard/weight_trend): interpolated scale data and a recent-weighted moving average underpin its trend. Its exact smoothing implementation is not specified here.
- [Program changes](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/242-create-a-new-macro-program): changing a program does not reset expenditure learning.
- [Cut/bulk adjustments](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/222-how-does-macrofactor-make-adjustments-for-a-weight-gain-or-weight-loss-goal): goals and expenditure drive the energy budget; protein tracks body composition/training, with most calorie adjustments in carbs/fat. Our first version uses a simpler body-weight protein preference.
- [Dynamic maintenance](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/125-how-does-dynamic-maintenance-work-in-macrofactor): a roughly 0.7 kg band and gentle 0.15% weekly corrections guide users toward their chosen maintenance weight.
- [Logging frequency](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/110-how-frequently-do-i-need-to-log-my-nutrition-for-the-expenditure-algorithm-and-weekly-coaching-updates): MacroFactor supports some missing intake with its own estimation; it does not require 21 perfect days. We use observed contiguous intervals instead of claiming equivalent missing-day estimation.
- [Check-ins](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/247-introduction-to-check-ins-and-coaching-modules): weekly program recommendations plus focused data-review modules.
- [Timeline configuration](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/218-how-to-configure-your-food-timeline) and [logging to an hour](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/50-how-to-log-to-any-hour-on-the-timeline): time is the diary's organizing axis, with configurable presentation.
- [Full feature catalog](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/collections/18-macrofactor-nutrition): feature inventory for the remaining gaps above.

Macro Track's formulas, thresholds and validation are documented separately in `coaching.md`. This research does not establish equivalence to MacroFactor's physiological model or validation.
